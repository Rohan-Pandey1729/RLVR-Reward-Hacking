E10 at ten seeds; fix all 28 findings from the technical-review audit

E10, the decisive control, now runs at ten seeds instead of three, balanced across
every condition and all at 400 steps. The comparison is now reported as the PAIRED
difference in slope between the shared model and its per-prompt copies on the same
seed, which is the correct statistic and avoids reading two marginal intervals
against each other: +0.0191 per doubling, 95% CI [+0.0082, +0.0330], t = 2.82 on
ten paired seeds. The copies' own slope is +0.0019, CI [-0.0008, +0.0050], which is
consistent with zero; the shared model's is +0.0210, CI [+0.0094, +0.0369].

Blocking errors the audit found and this commit fixes.
  The abstract claimed the shared policy shows "no slope either" in both mask-only
  controls, which the paper's own printed CI refutes for E9 (+0.0056, CI excludes
  zero). It now says "no MORE slope than the per-prompt arm", which is what the
  paired comparison supports.

  E9 and E9b still compare against a tabular table and remove a different confound.

  ALIGNMENT between the SFT target and the verifier's mask. Reworded throughout,
  including the abstract's necessary condition.

  MaskOnlyVerifier docstring both said it falls. Corrected in both, with a note that
  the true direction biases E9b's slope downward.
